01 — About

Understand the data. Solve the real problem.

I enjoy working through the data engineering problems that are not straightforward: systems that describe the same entity differently, transformation rules that need careful reasoning, and integrations that must hold up in production.

My experience combines hands-on SQL and ETL development with solution architecture, metadata-driven ingestion, data modeling, data quality frameworks, and technical leadership. At Commonwealth Care Alliance, I helped build its Teradata Enterprise Data Platform and led Provider transformation work for a claims migration to Facets. At CareSource, I guided Provider migration, retention, and legacy integration retirement after the CCA acquisition.

Much of that work has been in healthcare payer data, but the engineering principles are transferable across industries. I'm now extending my foundation into the Databricks ecosystem through certifications in Data Engineering and Generative AI.
